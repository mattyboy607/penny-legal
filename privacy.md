## Information We Collect
- TikTok authorization tokens (access and refresh tokens) that you explicitly grant when connecting your account.

## How We Use Information
Information is used solely to:

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising.

## Data Storage and Retention
Tokens and related data are stored only as long as needed to operate the Service. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok account settings (Settings → Security → Apps and websites). Upon revocation, we delete stored tokens.
